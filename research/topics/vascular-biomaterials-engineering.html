    Study context and source

Reviewed answer from this paper: Yes. The stressed HUVECs contacted bare NiTi, NPH, NPH with control EVs or NPH with engineered EVs. Bare NiTi without RSL3 served as the baseline. The engineered coating gave a higher live-cell percentage and lower C11-BODIPY oxidation than the control-EV coating (n = 3 for the quantified assays).
