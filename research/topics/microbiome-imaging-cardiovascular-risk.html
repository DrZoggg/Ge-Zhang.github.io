What can microbiome-plus-imaging research contribute to cardiovascular endotyping?

微生物组与影像结合的研究能为心血管内表型研究提供什么？

Multimodal Artificial Intelligence for Microbiome-Informed Precision Cardiovascular Medicine

Cardiac Research · 2026 · Article

Reviewed answer from this paper: Microbial and metabolomic information describes upstream biology, whereas echocardiography, CT, MRI and vascular imaging define downstream organ phenotypes. Linking them may help identify reproducible plaque or cardiac-remodeling subgroups. The review identifies endotyping as a useful near-term direction, while requiring independent replication and meaningful incremental value before a model supports clinical risk or treatment decisions. Figure 3 explains complementarity; Figure 4 places integration within a proposed clinical evaluation and feedback cycle.

Which design and validation checks make a multimodal cardiovascular model convincing?

哪些设计和验证检查有助于评估多模态心血管模型？

Multimodal Artificial Intelligence for Microbiome-Informed Precision Cardiovascular Medicine

Cardiac Research · 2026 · Article

Reviewed answer from this paper: Control clinical modifiers and technical variation, respect microbiome compositionality and missingness, and guard against leakage and subgroup imbalance. Compare clinical, unimodal and simple multimodal models on the same partitions before claiming benefit from higher-capacity AI. Report calibration, uncertainty, subgroup stability and decision utility alongside discrimination. Test transport across populations, sites, assays and clinical settings, including missing modalities. Feature-importance explanations can aid interpretation but do not establish biological mechanism.
