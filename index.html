Cardiovascular research · Zhengzhou University

Ge Zhang 张格

张格（Ge Zhang），心血管医学与计算生物学研究者，研究方向包括人工智能、多组学、动脉粥样硬化、心力衰竭与昼夜节律。

Cardiovascular researcher focusing on artificial intelligence, multimodal clinical data, multi-omics, and circadian biology. My research spans basic, mechanistic, and translational cardiovascular science, with particular interests in explainable AI and clinical risk prediction, atherosclerosis and vascular biology, single-cell and multi-omics approaches, translational biomarker discovery, circadian disruption, heart failure, and precision cardiovascular medicine.
