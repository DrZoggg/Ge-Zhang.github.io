Counting note

The reported enrollment is consistent with (10+8) AAA patients and (5+4) controls. Protein measurements and repeated assays are not new participants. No independence from participants in other publications is inferred. External validation means assay and ROC assessment in 8 cases and 4 controls, not a prospective screening study. Specimen and isolation descriptions differ between source sections.

Research Question

Can targeted circulating extracellular-vesicle protein profiling identify AAA-associated candidates that merit external assay evaluation, and how consistent are their signals across Western blot, ELISA and ROC analyses?

Author Evidence Summary

This two-center human study enrolled 18 AAA patients and 9 healthy controls: 10 cases and 5 controls in the in-house cohort, and 8 cases and 4 controls in the external cohort. A targeted Olink proximity-extension workflow assessed 92 EV-associated proteins and prioritized five candidates. External Western blot and ELISA did not show uniformly concordant findings: MCP-1 direction differed between the two reported analyses, and Neurturin was not significant by Western blot but lower by ELISA. The article reports single-marker ROC AUCs from 0.760 to 0.900 in the small external comparison, with wide confidence intervals. Different passages also describe venous plasma, serum EVs and intraoperative aortic blood; those specimen descriptions are not silently treated as one validated routine blood-screening protocol. The evidence is exploratory biomarker association and external assay assessment, not prospective AAA detection, rupture prediction or a therapeutic target validation.

What This Study Adds

  • A circulating EV-associated targeted-protein view of human AAA.
  • External Western blot and ELISA evaluation of five candidate proteins with the cross-assay differences retained.
  • Small-sample, single-marker ROC evidence with explicit uncertainty and no claim of a prospective screening tool.

Evidence Scope

Supports

  • AAA-associated targeted EV protein differences in the studied human samples.
  • Two-center enrollment and small external assay assessment.
  • Article-reported single-marker ROC discrimination with uncertainty.
  • Computational inflammatory/pathway interpretation of the candidate set.

Does Not Establish

  • A whole-proteome survey or equivalence to unfractionated plasma protein profiling.
  • Concordant directional validation of every candidate across Western blot and ELISA.
  • A validated five-protein combined diagnostic model.
  • Prospective incident-AAA, rupture or mortality prediction.
  • An established routine peripheral-venous blood screening protocol across all source descriptions.
  • Causal pathway regulation or a therapeutically validated protein target.

Limitations

  • The external cohort contains 8 cases and 4 controls; ROC confidence intervals are wide, including 0.486-1.000 for the reported AUC of 0.760.
  • MCP-1 and Neurturin do not have uniformly concordant Western blot and ELISA results. No cause of these differences is established by this evidence page.
  • Venous-plasma, serum-EV and intraoperative-aortic-blood descriptions appear in different sections, and isolation methods differ between discovery and external-validation descriptions.
  • The targeted 92-protein panel does not establish an unbiased circulating proteome.
  • Case-control ROC discrimination and pathway enrichment do not establish prospective prediction, clinical screening utility or causality.

Q&A

How many participants were studied?

The article reports 18 AAA patients and 9 controls: 10/5 in-house and 8/4 external. These numbers describe the two reported cohorts, not additional participants per assay.

Evidence: KF1

Was this unbiased plasma proteomics?

No. The study examined an isolated circulating vesicle-associated fraction with a targeted 92-protein Olink panel. Specimen wording also differs between source sections.

Evidence: KF2, KF3

Which proteins were prioritized?

IL-4, IL-6, MCP-1, Neurturin and Oncostatin-M. They are candidate biomarkers in the analyzed samples, not approved diagnostic markers.

Evidence: KF3

Did all five proteins replicate consistently in external assays?

No. Western blot and ELISA differed for MCP-1 direction and Neurturin significance/direction. The evidence should be cited as assay-dependent external evaluation, not uniformly concordant validation.

Evidence: KF4

What ROC performance was reported?

In the article-listed order IL-4, IL-6, MCP-1, Neurturin and Oncostatin-M, AUCs were 0.760, 0.840, 0.800, 0.840 and 0.900. The external group was small and confidence intervals were wide. These are single-marker comparisons, not a combined five-protein model.

Evidence: KF5

Does the study prove that these proteins predict rupture or are treatment targets?

No. It reports association, small external assay/ROC comparisons and computational annotations, not prospective rupture prediction or therapeutic target validation.

Evidence: KF4, KF5, KF6
