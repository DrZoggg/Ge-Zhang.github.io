## Official Abstract

As one of the most prevalent heritable cardiovascular diseases, dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) induces cardiac insufficiency and dysfunction. Although genetic mutation has been identified one of the causes of DCM, the usage of genetic biomarkers such as RNAs for DCM early diagnosis is still being overlooked. In addition, the alternation of RNAs could reflect the progression of the diseases, as an indicator for the prognosis of patients. Therefore, it is beneficial to develop genetic based diagnostic tool for DCM. RNAs are often unstable within circulatory system, leading to the infeasibility for clinical application. Recently discovered exosomal miRNAs have the stability that is then need for diagnostic purpose. Hence, fully understanding of the exosomal miRNA within DCM patients is vital for clinical translation. In this study, we employed the next generation sequencing based on the plasma exosomal miRNAs to comprehensively characterize the miRNAs expression in plasma exosomes from DCM patients exhibiting chronic heart failure (CHF) compared to healthy individuals. A complex landscape of differential miRNAs and target genes in DCM with CHF patients were identified. More importantly, we discovered that 92 differentially expressed miRNAs in DCM patients undergoing CHF were correlated with several enriched pathways, including oxytocin signalling pathway, circadian entrainment, hippo signalling pathway‐multiple species, ras signalling pathway and morphine addiction. This study reveals the miRNA expression profiles in plasma exosomes in DCM patients with CHF, and further reveal their potential roles in the pathogenesis of it, presenting a new direction for clinical diagnosis and management of DCM patients with CHF.

## Research Question

Which plasma exosomal miRNA expression patterns distinguish the studied DCM-with-chronic-heart-failure samples from healthy controls, and which candidate signals were evaluated by an external assay?

## Author Evidence Summary

This human case-control molecular-profiling study used plasma vesicle preparations, small-RNA sequencing, target prediction and external qRT-PCR to investigate DCM with chronic heart failure. The Results report 92 differentially expressed miRNA features, comprising 48 upregulated and 44 downregulated features under the stated screening thresholds. Computational enrichment and network analyses provide biological hypotheses rather than directly measured target regulation. Ten selected hub miRNAs were assessed in an external group of 10 patients and 6 healthy controls, with expression-profile separation reported. The discovery participant count was not reliably established from the reviewed text and is not reconstructed here. miR-185-5p appears in the discovery table and predicted network, but not in the listed 10-miRNA external qRT-PCR panel. The evidence supports exploratory circulating-RNA associations and assay follow-up, not an early-detection test, prospective prognosis or a human causal mechanism.

## Study Design & Analytical Framework

### Study profile

- Study Design: Human case-control plasma exosomal miRNA discovery with external qRT-PCR follow-up
- Evidence Type: Human molecular association and small external assay evaluation
- Population: Patients with DCM and chronic heart failure compared with healthy individuals; recruitment criteria included LVEF <=45%, NYHA II-IV and heart-failure history longer than 6 months
- Primary Endpoint: Between-group plasma exosomal miRNA expression differences
- Secondary Endpoint: Predicted target/pathway relationships and external hub-miRNA expression-profile evaluation
- External dataset evaluation: Yes
- Data modalities: plasma vesicle preparation and characterization, small-RNA sequencing, external qRT-PCR, target prediction and pathway enrichment, network analysis and PCoA

## What This Study Adds

- A plasma vesicle-associated small-RNA expression resource for DCM with chronic heart failure.
- A reported 92-feature differential set with computational target and pathway hypotheses.
- External expression-assay assessment of 10 hub miRNAs, with a clear distinction from the discovery-stage miR-185-5p finding.

## Evidence Scope

### Supports

- DCM-HF-associated plasma exosomal miRNA expression differences in the studied samples.
- An article-reported 92-feature screen and computational miRNA-target/pathway prioritization.
- Small external qRT-PCR evaluation of a selected 10-miRNA panel.
- Discovery-stage inclusion of hsa-miR-185-5p with the Table 1 values reported above.

### Does Not Establish

- A prospectively validated early diagnostic test or prognostic model for DCM.
- Causation of human DCM by the differential miRNAs or their predicted targets.
- External qRT-PCR validation of miR-185-5p in this article.
- Direct biochemical binding for every predicted miRNA-mRNA interaction.
- Cardiac-cell origin or exclusive exosomal origin of every measured circulating RNA.
- An effective miRNA-directed treatment in humans.

### Limitations

- The discovery participant count is not reliably established from the reviewed text; no synthetic total is supplied.
- The final Results screen uses absolute logFC >0.5, while Fig. 3A prints >0.05. P-value and FDR statements are not assumed to establish the same feature-wise threshold.
- Methods 2.9 includes an abdominal-aortic-wall/mRNA extraction passage inconsistent with the surrounding plasma-exosomal-miRNA assay description; this is retained as a reporting limitation, not reinterpreted as a myocardial tissue experiment.
- External evaluation is small (10 cases and 6 controls), and multivariate separation is not prospective diagnostic validation.
- Some feature labels retain homolog/isomiR-style naming in the source; the complete feature set is not relabeled as uniquely verified canonical human mature miRNAs.

## Q&A

### What kind of DCM evidence does this paper provide?

Human case-control plasma miRNA expression profiling with an external assay phase; it is not a treatment trial or prospective prediction study.

Evidence: KF1

### How many differential miRNA features were reported?

The final Results screen reports 92 features, split into 48 upregulated and 44 downregulated, using absolute logFC >0.5 and P<0.05. Fig. 3A prints a different logFC threshold; this discrepancy is not silently reconciled.

Evidence: KF3

### Were the predicted pathways and miRNA targets experimentally established?

No. Target, enrichment and network analyses nominate relationships; the 55-node, 59-edge miRNA-mRNA network does not establish direct target binding or pathway causality.

Evidence: KF4

### What was evaluated in the external population?

Ten selected hub miRNAs were assessed by qRT-PCR in 10 DCM patients and 6 healthy controls. Expression-distance and PCoA analyses supported group differences, not clinical diagnostic utility.

Evidence: KF5

### Was miR-185-5p one of the externally tested hub miRNAs?

No. It appears in Table 1 with logFC 1.04 and P=0.0322 and in the predicted-network discussion, but is absent from the 10-miRNA external qRT-PCR list.

Evidence: KF6

### Can the results be treated as a measured whole-plasma or myocardial tissue signature?

No. The reported study concerns isolated plasma vesicle-associated RNA. It does not establish myocardial origin or interchangeability with whole-plasma RNA measurements.

Evidence: KF1, KF2
