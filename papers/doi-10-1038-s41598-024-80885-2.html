Research question and experimental setting

The paper combines cancer-expression analyses with colorectal-cancer cell experiments involving SLC7A7. The inspected assays use HCT116 and SW480 cells, with RNA interference and measurements of migration, invasion and molecular expression. These are experimental cell-model observations, not evidence that targeting SLC7A7 benefits patients.

Source: Methods: Cell culture; RNA interference; cell migration/invasion methods; Figure 5A–E

What the inspected figures support

Figure 5A–E reports reduced migration and invasion after SLC7A7 knockdown in the two cell lines. Figure 6C identifies APC as the measured analyte and shows increased APC expression after knockdown. These limited observations can be distinguished from the paper’s broader RNA-modification mechanism; no animal sample size is inferred from displayed examples.

Source: Figure 5A–E; Figure 6C; Animal models and Figure 5F

Unresolved molecular identity

The official title says N7-methyladenosine, whereas the abstract and mechanistic assays refer to N7-methylguanosine/m7G. A complete mechanism-centred V2 cannot silently choose or rename the modification. This disclosure preserves the formal title while withholding endorsement of the disputed mechanistic identity.

Source: Title versus Abstract; mechanistic Results; Figure 6E–I; Supplementary Figure 4
