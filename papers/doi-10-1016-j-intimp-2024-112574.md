## Research context and source limits

Limited evidence note. Only the source material identified below has been reviewed; this is not a complete Paper GEO V2 assessment.

Abstract-supported computational and animal-study context; full model evaluation and experimental records remain unverified.

### Computational research question

The abstract describes analysis of public ischemic cardiomyopathy expression datasets, comparison of machine-learning models, and immune-feature analyses. It names DLST, ATP7B, FDX1, SLC31A1 and DLAT as the candidate panel examined by the authors.

Source: [PubMed PMID 38971104, Abstract: Methods and Results, core-gene panel.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38971104/)

### Experimental component

The Methods describe a mouse model created by left anterior descending coronary artery ligation, with Western blot and immunohistochemistry to examine candidate-gene expression. This provides an experimental setting for further review; it is not a prospective human diagnostic validation.

Source: [PubMed PMID 38971104, Abstract: Methods, LAD ligation and WB/IHC.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38971104/)

### Evidence limits

- The complete methods and supplements are needed to verify sample overlap, model-selection nesting, diagnostic endpoints and animal group sizes. Reported AUCs, docking scores and chemical predictions are not adopted here as verified diagnostic or therapeutic evidence.
