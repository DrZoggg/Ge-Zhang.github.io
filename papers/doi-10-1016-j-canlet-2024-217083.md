## Research context and source limits

Abstract-supported safety-review context; no incidence estimate or causal mechanism is established here.

### Safety-review question

This 2024 review was prompted by regulatory reports of T-cell malignancies, including CAR-positive lymphomas, after BCMA- or CD19-targeted autologous CAR-T therapy. The abstract identifies clinical trials and post-marketing adverse-event data as the origins of those reports.

Source: [PubMed PMID 38925363, Abstract: reported regulatory safety context; publication type Review.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38925363/)

### Interpretation boundary

The review asks about possible mechanisms behind secondary T-cell cancers. Reports occurring after treatment do not by themselves prove treatment causality, quantify the absolute risk, or establish one mechanism for every case.

Source: [PubMed PMID 38925363, Abstract: final sentence stating the mechanistic review objective.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38925363/)

### Evidence limits

- The complete review and the underlying case, molecular and surveillance records are needed to assess denominators, CAR positivity and alternative explanations. The historical abstract should not be treated as a current regulatory recommendation or clinical management guideline.
