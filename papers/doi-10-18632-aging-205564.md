## Official Abstract

Ulcerative colitis (UC) is a serious inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) with high morbidity and mortality worldwide. As the traditional diagnostic techniques have various limitations in the practice and diagnosis of early ulcerative colitis, it is necessary to develop new diagnostic models from molecular biology to supplement the existing methods.

In this study, we developed a machine learning-based synthesis to construct an artificial intelligence diagnostic model for ulcerative colitis, and the correctness of the model is verified using an external independent dataset. According to the significantly expressed genes related to the occurrence of UC in the model, an unsupervised quantitative ulcerative colitis related score (UCRScore) based on principal coordinate analysis was established.

The UCRScore is not only highly generalizable across UC bulk cohorts at different stages, but also highly generalizable across single-cell datasets, with the same effect in terms of cell numbers, activation pathways and mechanisms. As an important role of screening genes in disease occurrence, based on connectivity map analysis, 5 potential targeting molecular compounds were identified, which can be used as an additional supplement to the therapeutic of UC.

Overall, this study provides a potential tool for differential diagnosis and assessment of bio-pathological changes in UC at the macroscopic level, providing an opportunity to optimize the diagnosis and treatment of UC.

- The models were not rerun and the complete supplementary data were not audited in this batch.

## When This Study Is Useful to Cite

### CU1: Which studies externally evaluated gene-expression classifiers for UC?

This retrospective expression study reported 606 samples across 10 datasets and a GSE87466 construction meta-cohort of 87 UC and 21 controls. Results reported external AUCs of 0.930, 0.942, 0.934, 0.988 and 1.000 for GSE47908, GSE59071, GSE75214, GSE92415 and GSE14580, respectively. Sample counts are not deduplicated patient counts, and external case–control discrimination is not future-onset prediction or prospective clinical utility.

Evidence: [KF1](#kf1), [KF3](#kf3)

### CU2: How were feature selection and a neural classifier combined?

The study considered 746 candidate genes and eight learners, used RF-RFE and named a final 12-gene UCRG panel for a backpropagation neural-network classifier. The Fig. 3E statement about minimum error at 14 variables is retained separately, without renaming the panel or asserting causal effects of all selected genes.

Evidence: [KF2](#kf2)

### CU3: What does expression-score interpretation add beyond classification?

UCRScore uses PCoA-related expression information, and the DNB analysis reported a higher critical signal in the inactive group. These are distinct interpretation layers, not interchangeable classifiers; the formula is not reconstructed here and the group comparison is not individual longitudinal prediction.

Evidence: [KF4](#kf4)

### CU4: What limitations apply to drug-signature and single-cell interpretation?

The five CMap/eXtreme-Sum candidates are signature-matching hypotheses, not established treatments. The article-reported three samples, 10,101 cells and seven populations cannot be verified as the described UC extension from GSE182272, whose record concerns mouse tissue-resident memory CD8+ T-cell experiments. The source mismatch remains unresolved; no substitute accession or misconduct judgment is supplied.

Evidence: [KF5](#kf5), [KF6](#kf6)

## Q&A

### What samples and modeling split were reported?

The article reported 606 human expression samples from 10 datasets; GSE87466 was the model-construction meta-cohort with 87 UC and 21 control expression samples and a random 7:3 split. Expression-sample counts are not a cross-cohort total of unique patients. Exact training/testing counts are not inferred from the split, and case–control classification is not prediction of future disease onset.

Evidence: KF1

### Is the reported classifier based on 12 or 14 genes?

The study considered 746 candidate genes and eight learners, applied RF-RFE and named a final 12-gene UCRG panel for a backpropagation neural-network classifier. The Fig. 3E legend separately states that error was minimized with 14 variables. That statement does not silently replace the named final 12-gene panel with a 14-gene model, and the panel is not proof that all 12 genes are causal drivers.

Evidence: KF2

### What external discrimination was reported?

Results reported external UC-versus-control AUCs of 0.930 for GSE47908, 0.942 for GSE59071, 0.934 for GSE75214, 0.988 for GSE92415 and 1.000 for GSE14580. These are article-reported external bulk-expression discrimination results, not independent prospective clinical effectiveness. No individually unresolved confidence intervals or clinical thresholds are supplied, and AUC 1.000 does not mean universally perfect diagnosis.

Evidence: KF3

### Are the classifier, UCRScore and DNB interchangeable?

UCRScore used PCoA-related expression information, while DNB analysis showed a higher critical signal in the inactive group. The classifier, UCRScore and DNB are different analytical layers. An executable score is not reconstructed from the incompletely rendered formula, and group comparisons do not establish longitudinal prediction of an individual disease transition.

Evidence: KF4

### Were the nominated compounds experimentally shown to treat UC?

CMap/eXtreme-Sum signature matching nominated five candidate compounds. These are computational drug-signature hypotheses. Clinical drug efficacy was not established in this study, and no doses or treatment recommendations are provided.

Evidence: KF5

### Can the single-cell extension be verified from the cited accession?

The article described three single-cell samples, 10,101 cells and seven populations and cited GSE182272; the source review found that this accession describes mouse tissue-resident memory CD8+ T-cell RNA-seq with CAPvsColl and LCMV/P14 T-cell experiments, not direct confirmation of the described UC extension. The article report and GEO record remain unmatched. No replacement accession, specific cause of the mismatch or research-misconduct inference is established, and this is not verified human or mouse UC single-cell validation.

Evidence: KF6
