Author evidence note

Principal contribution

This review connects biomedical data provenance and endpoint design with model selection, causal interpretation, validation, and deployment governance. Its disease-agnostic framework includes an evidence ladder for positioning AI findings and a proposed model-and-data card for reproducible reporting. It synthesizes methods across omics, imaging/pathology, clinical records, and longitudinal data to guide trustworthy research and translation.

Source: Introduction, manuscript lines 121-133, physical p.9; reporting/model-and-data-card, lines 666-679, physical p.34; evidence ladder, lines 491-518, physical pp.26-27.

Evidence ladder

The review organizes support for an AI finding into association, robustness, biological coherence, causal support, perturbation evidence, and clinical utility. The ladder helps researchers identify what additional evidence is needed to move a finding toward a biological explanation or clinical application.

Source: Evidence ladder, manuscript lines 491–518, physical PDF pages 26–27.

Research use

The framework can inform the design and reporting of studies that combine biomedical data types. Its proposed model-and-data card gives researchers a place to document data provenance, model choices and validation conditions alongside the intended use.

Source: Introduction, manuscript lines 121–133, physical PDF page 9; reporting and model-and-data card, lines 666–679, page 34.
