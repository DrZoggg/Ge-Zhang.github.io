## Evidence Scale

**IPPS: retrospective LUAD prognostic modeling and its validation limits**

A 13-gene LUAD prognostic model was selected from 101 algorithm combinations using public transcriptomic cohorts. Gene screening and model selection reused evaluation cohorts; survival-defined groups and unresolved reporting discrepancies prevent treating this study as independent immunotherapy-response validation.

| Evidence | Value |
| --- | --- |
| final signature genes | 13 |
| candidate model combinations | 101 |
| GEO cohorts used in screening and model selection | 5 |

### Counting note

Methods 2.1 reports 577 LUAD cases from OAK/POPLAR, whereas Results 3.1 and Figure 1D describe a selected group of 296. The retrieved article does not fully reconcile those denominators; no pooled participant count is inferred. TCGA and five GEO cohorts entered survival-based gene screening, and the five GEO cohorts also determined model selection. They are distinct datasets, not independent held-out validation after model selection.

## Research Question

How was an immunotherapy-associated gene signature developed for retrospective LUAD survival stratification, and what limits the interpretation of its reported performance?

## Author Evidence Summary

The study used OAK/POPLAR data for an initial expression contrast and screened candidate genes against survival in TCGA-LUAD and five GEO cohorts. It constructed 101 model combinations in TCGA and selected Lasso plus SuperPC using the mean C-index across the five GEO cohorts. The final IPPS includes 13 genes. The TCGA time-dependent AUCs reported consistently in the text and Figure 2H are 0.80, 0.79 and 0.78 at 1, 3 and 5 years; these are training-cohort estimates. The GEO datasets contributed to gene screening and model selection, so they are not untouched independent tests. The initial groups were defined using overall-survival thresholds despite being labelled CR/PR and PD/SD. Drug-sensitivity results are computational hypotheses. Differences in the reported OAK/POPLAR denominators and in GSE31210 AUCs remain unresolved.

## Study Design & Analytical Framework

### Study profile

- Study Design: Retrospective public-cohort gene screening and survival-model selection with computational immune and drug analyses
- Evidence Type: Retrospective transcriptomic associations and model-development performance
- Population: LUAD records from TCGA, five GEO datasets and an OAK/POPLAR-derived discovery comparison
- Primary Endpoint: Overall survival association and time-dependent discrimination
- Secondary Endpoint: Inferred immune infiltration, genomic associations and computational drug sensitivity
- External dataset evaluation: No
- Data modalities: bulk transcriptomics, clinical survival data, mutation and copy-number data, computational immune and drug-response estimates

## What This Study Adds

- A documented 13-gene prognostic-model development workflow linking an OAK/POPLAR discovery contrast to TCGA and GEO survival analyses.
- A case for separating retrospective prognostic discrimination, outcome-driven model selection and computational treatment hypotheses from independent clinical validation.

## Evidence Scope

### Supports

- Description of the reported 13-gene Lasso-plus-SuperPC development procedure and 101 candidate combinations.
- The text-and-figure-consistent TCGA training AUCs, explicitly labelled as apparent development-cohort performance.
- A source-based distinction between survival-defined discovery groups, prognostic modeling and computational drug-response inference.

### Does Not Establish

- An independently validated predictor of RECIST response or differential benefit from immunotherapy.
- Prospective clinical utility or improved outcomes from IPPS-guided treatment.
- Unbiased external performance after gene and algorithm selection using the same GEO cohorts.
- A reconciled OAK/POPLAR denominator or a resolved GSE31210 AUC estimate.
- Experimental drug efficacy or causal functions of the model genes.

### Limitations

- The study is retrospective. Survival-based gene selection used all six TCGA/GEO cohorts, and five GEO cohorts were reused to choose the algorithm. Their results cannot be treated as untouched external validation.
- Methods 2.1 states 577 OAK/POPLAR LUAD cases, while Results 3.1 and Figure 1D state a selected 296. The retrieved version does not sufficiently specify the full filtering relationship; no reconciled or pooled denominator is asserted.
- Results 3.1 and Figure 1D use OS >12 months and OS <6 months but name the groups CR/PR and PD/SD. Survival strata do not establish objective response categories or differential immunotherapy benefit.
- GSE31210 AUCs differ between Results 3.2 (0.67, 0.76, 0.72 at 1, 3, 5 years) and Figure 2L (0.67, 0.73, 0.75). Neither series is adopted as a verified performance estimate.
- The TCGA AUCs quoted here are apparent training-cohort estimates. Individual participant data and code were not reanalysed, and no uncertainty interval is invented.
- Drug-response analyses are computational. The Discussion uses experimental language, but Methods 2.4 and Results 3.7 describe inference from drug-sensitivity databases.
- The author-uploaded publisher-layout text was readable publicly on ResearchGate, and Figures 1 and 2 were visually checked there. Direct PDF download was blocked in this environment; the archived source fingerprint identifies the acquired web transcription, not PDF bytes. No claim is based on an unseen supplementary result.

## Q&A

### What model did the IPPS study select?

A 13-gene Lasso-plus-SuperPC model was selected from 101 candidate combinations. The five GEO evaluation cohorts also contributed to gene screening and algorithm selection.

Evidence: KF1

### What survival performance can be quoted without resolving the GSE31210 discrepancy?

The text and Figure 2H agree on TCGA AUCs of 0.80, 0.79 and 0.78 at 1, 3 and 5 years. They describe the development cohort and must not be presented as independent external performance.

Evidence: KF2

### Were the five GEO cohorts untouched independent test sets?

No. Results 3.1 uses the six TCGA/GEO cohorts for survival-based gene screening, and Results 3.2 selects the final algorithm using the mean C-index across the five GEO cohorts.

Evidence: KF1

### Does the OAK/POPLAR analysis verify an objective immunotherapy-response classifier?

No. The reported contrast uses overall survival greater than 12 months versus less than 6 months, despite CR/PR and PD/SD labels. This does not establish independently verified objective-response prediction or treatment benefit.

Evidence: KF3

### Were the proposed drugs tested as IPPS-guided treatments?

The reported analysis uses oncoPredict, pRRophetic, CTRP and PRISM to infer drug sensitivity. It does not demonstrate patient benefit from selecting a drug using IPPS.

Evidence: KF4
