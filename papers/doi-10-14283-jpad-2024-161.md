## Evidence Scale

**Shift work and AD: seven molecular candidates, blood assays and clock-deviation scoring**

The study integrates shift-work and AD genetic/expression data to prioritize seven genes and proposes clock deviation level (CDL), an expression score built against matched random-gene backgrounds. It complements those computational analyses with local blood-expression, serum-protein and sleep/biomarker observations.

| Evidence | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Shift-work GWAS | 263,315 European-ancestry individuals, as reported |
| AD GWAS | 63,926 individuals: 21,982 cases and 41,944 controls, as reported |
| eQTLGen resource | 31,684 healthy Europeans, as reported |
| Expression data | 4,077 sample entries: 2,241 AD and 1,836 controls across 16 dataset/platform groups |
| Gene selection | 464 overlapping genes; 220 concordant candidates; 7 selected target genes |
| Local clinical recruitment | 45 AD and 12 healthy participants |
| Published assay table rows | Table S7 qPCR: 4 AD/4 NC; Table S8 ELISA: 16 AD/8 NC |
| CDL background construction | 50 expression bins; 1,000 matched random samplings |

### Counting note

GWAS participants, eQTL-resource participants, expression entries and local participants are different units and are not added into a total. The 16 expression groups include three platform entries for GSE84422; 4,077 entries are not an independently deduplicated patient count. The local 45/12 recruitment is separate from the smaller rows in the qPCR/ELISA tables; their sample-selection links and biological-versus-technical replication are incompletely specified. The additional datasets contribute group comparisons for the expression-derived CDL score.

## Research Question

Which circadian-related molecular candidates connect the study’s shift-work and AD analyses, how is a transcriptomic clock-deviation score constructed, and what expression and biomarker patterns accompany those candidates?

## Author Evidence Summary

The study combines shift-work GWAS, AD GWAS, cis-eQTL data and 4,077 reported expression entries across 16 dataset/platform groups. A 464-gene overlap is reduced to 220 concordant candidates and seven target genes: CCS, CDS2, MYRIP, NRP1, PLEKHA5, POLR1D and PPP4C. The separate local clinical component includes 45 people with AD and 12 healthy participants. Its published assay tables contain four AD/four control qPCR rows and sixteen AD/eight control ELISA rows, with higher CCS/POLR1D/PPP4C mRNA, lower NRP1/PLEKHA5 mRNA, and corresponding selected serum-protein/AD-marker differences. Sleep-questionnaire scores correlate with hippocampal volume and measured biomarkers. CDL uses seven-gene expression, within-dataset centering, 50 expression bins and 1,000 matched-background samplings; the article reports distributions and comparisons in multiple expression datasets. Reported CDL correlations are r=-0.30 with alpha-secretase activity and r=-0.33 with soluble Aβ42. The principal MR interval/instrument-count disagreement and the GSE124870 species/condition mapping remain explicit at their affected claims. The contribution is an integrated candidate/scoring framework and complementary molecular/clinical observations.

## Study Design & Analytical Framework

### Study profile

- Study Design: Two-sample shift-work-to-AD and gene-expression-to-AD MR analyses; public expression screening and seven-gene score construction; additional perturbation/sleep dataset comparisons; separate retrospective local clinical study with qPCR, serum ELISA, MRI and questionnaires.
- Evidence Type: GWAS/cis-eQTL association, public transcriptomics, published MR summaries, computational expression scoring, local molecular measurements and cross-sectional biomarker associations.
- Population: Article-reported European-ancestry GWAS/eQTL resources; public AD/control expression entries and separate reference experiments; local AD and healthy participants recruited at one hospital in 2023-2024.
- Primary Endpoint: Selection and characterization of seven circadian-related molecular candidates and construction of CDL.
- Secondary Endpoint: Reported shift-work/gene MR estimates; target-gene mRNA/protein directions; questionnaire, hippocampal and biomarker associations; score distribution/comparison and functional enrichment.
- External dataset evaluation: Yes
- Data modalities: GWAS summary statistics, Cis-eQTL summary statistics, Public microarray/transcriptomic expression, Local qPCR and serum ELISA, Hippocampal MRI measurements, MEQ-SA/PSQI questionnaires, Expression-derived CDL and pathway/network analyses

## What This Study Adds

- Connects shift-work-associated and AD expression screens with cis-eQTL association to prioritize seven concrete molecular candidates.
- Adds local qPCR, serum ELISA and sleep/hippocampal-biomarker observations with their distinct assay and participant units visible.
- Defines an expression-background CDL construction and presents multiple dataset comparisons, enabling comparison of score design, biological context and validation requirements.

## Evidence Scope

### Supports

- The study’s seven-gene selection process and source-specific gene-expression MR associations.
- Local mRNA/serum-protein directions and questionnaire/biomarker correlations as cross-sectional observations.
- The specified CDL construction, reported expression-group comparisons and associated enrichment hypotheses.

### Does Not Establish

- A resolved causal shift-work effect size from the inconsistent printed intervals/instrument counts.
- A clinically validated individual circadian-disruption diagnostic threshold or prospective AD prevention benefit.
- The GSE124870 day-versus-night-worker interpretation, gene-mediated causation or experimentally proven treatment targets.

### Limitations

- The principal MR report has inconsistent OR/beta intervals and 15-versus-13 instrument counts. Supplementary sensitivity p values are 0.15-0.31; the causal magnitude is not resolved by the source.
- GSE124870 is rat heart under shift-work/feeding conditions in the accepted official SOFT, rather than the paper’s mice/day-versus-night-worker description; the Figure 5E mapping remains unresolved.
- Public expression entries, including three GSE84422 platform groups, are not a deduplicated patient census. Local qPCR/ELISA row counts and replicate definitions do not equal the full 45/12 recruitment.
- CDL uses within-matrix centering and expression-bin backgrounds. Distribution/cutoff similarities and retrospective group contrasts do not establish a physiological circadian gold standard or prospective individual diagnostic performance; one displayed AD comparison is nonsignificant.
- Clinical correlations are observational, with valid-pair counts or source mapping incomplete for some endpoints. The study does not perturb the candidates, establish biological mediation or test AD prevention/treatment.

## Q&A

### What data and sample units does this study combine?

Methods describes shift-work GWAS from 263,315 people, AD GWAS from 63,926 (21,982 cases/41,944 controls), eQTLGen data from 31,684 people, 4,077 public expression entries and a separate 45-AD/12-healthy local clinical component. These resources are not added into a patient total. The 16 expression groups include three platform entries from GSE84422; their unique-patient independence was not reconstructed.

Evidence: KF1, KF2, KF3, KF4

### How were the seven molecular candidates selected?

The study reports 464 genes overlapping its AD-expression and shift-work-associated screens, then 220 with concordant directions. Cis-eQTL MR at FDR<0.1 and correlation with a circadian-rhythm GSVA score retained CCS, CDS2, MYRIP, NRP1, PLEKHA5, POLR1D and PPP4C.

Evidence: KF1, KF3

### What is the principal shift-work MR result and which discrepancies matter?

The article prints IVW OR 2.49 (95% CI 1.79-3.19), p=0.01. Figure 2 prints beta 0.91, SE 0.35 and beta interval 0.21-1.61, inconsistent with that OR interval. Results reports 15 final instruments. Figure 2D lists 13 SNP labels and Table S4 sensitivity analyses use 13 (p=0.15-0.31). The analyzed instrument set and OR interval remain unresolved.

Evidence: KF2

### What gene-expression MR associations were reported?

Figure 3B reports higher-odds associations for CCS, POLR1D and PPP4C and lower-odds associations for CDS2, MYRIP, NRP1 and PLEKHA5. The POLR1D IVW OR is 1.08 (95% CI 1.03-1.13), while MYRIP uses a Wald ratio. These estimates compare genetically proxied expression with AD odds. KF3 lists all seven intervals.

Evidence: KF3

### Which expression directions were observed in the local assays?

qPCR shows higher CCS/POLR1D/PPP4C and lower NRP1/PLEKHA5 in AD, while CDS2 and MYRIP are nonsignificant. Serum ELISA shows higher p-tau181/CCS/PPP4C and lower NRP1/Aβ1-42. Table S7 contains four AD/four NC qPCR rows; Table S8 contains sixteen AD/eight NC ELISA rows. These smaller assay tables are kept separate from the full 45/12 recruitment.

Evidence: KF4

### What clinical sleep and biomarker associations are available?

Table S10 reports PSQI negatively correlated with left/right hippocampal volumes (r=-0.633446/-0.6238633), positively with p-tau181 (r=0.50409385) and negatively with Aβ1-42 (r=-0.6004647). MEQ-SA correlates positively with left/right volumes (r=0.56260997/0.62576542). Valid-pair n is not explicitly supplied for each correlation. They describe questionnaire-associated clinical variation.

Evidence: KF5

### How is CDL constructed?

The expression matrix is centered gene by gene across the analyzed samples. Target genes are matched to random genes using 50 mean-expression bins and 1,000 samplings. CDL=L_random-L_TGs, where each component averages its centered expression features. The formula yields an expression score relative to the analyzed matrix.

Evidence: KF6

### What additional CDL evaluations are reported?

Figure 5D shows higher CDL in the GSE43073 Bmal1-knockout reference group (p=0.043), and 5F in sleep restriction versus extension in GSE82114 (p=0.025). Figure 5G displays six AD/control datasets; the GSE29378 comparison has p=0.064, while the other five displayed p values are below 0.05.

Evidence: KF7

### Why does the GSE124870 interpretation require a specific source note?

The paper calls it shift-work mice and Figure 5E labels Day SW/Night SW. Its already verified official GEO SOFT instead identifies rat heart under control, shift work and shift work with food restricted to night, sampled at six times. The figure’s group-to-condition mapping is therefore unresolved.

Evidence: KF7

### What do the CDL-biomarker and functional analyses add?

Figure 5H-I reports CDL correlations of r=-0.30 with alpha-secretase activity (p=0.026) and r=-0.33 with soluble Aβ42 (p=0.014). Figure S2 adds target-gene interaction/over-representation context; Figure S3 adds immune, inflammatory and metabolic/respiratory enrichment. These are candidate mechanisms and associations, with incomplete assay/sample mapping for the two CDL-biomarker plots.

Evidence: KF8
