Research question and reported workflow

The study investigates disulfidptosis-related expression patterns in pulmonary hypertension. Its workflow compares random forest, support-vector machine, generalized linear and XGBoost models, then constructs a nomogram and regulatory-network hypotheses. DSTN, NDUFS1, RPN1, TLN1 and MYH10 are the five nominated genes; their nomination does not establish a clinically validated diagnostic panel.

Source: Methods: Construction of machine learning models; Results: Construction and evaluation of SVM, RF, GLM and XGB machine models

Separate experimental component

The paper also reports a hypoxia-exposure rodent experiment and Western-blot measurements of the nominated proteins. This is a preclinical expression assessment, separate from human diagnostic-model evaluation. It does not establish diagnostic accuracy in patients, demonstrate a treatment effect, or validate predicted drugs from molecular docking.

Source: Methods: Animal; Chemicals and materials; Results: Five DiGs as diagnostic markers were validated in HPH animal model; Figure 8

Why performance remains unverified

The abstract and Methods list different training and validation accessions. The Results also assign validation AUCs to cohorts differently from the Figure 5 caption. Neither accession series nor curve attribution is silently selected here; the cohort-to-analysis mapping requires clarification.

Source: Abstract Methods; Materials and methods: Data source; validation Results; Figure 5D–I caption

Evidence limits

  • No disputed AUC is presented as verified.
  • Protein-expression checks do not resolve the human cohort identity conflicts.
