Research Question

Which disulfidptosis-related expression candidates characterize PH, how do they relate to molecular clusters and immune/pathway patterns, and which protein directions recur in a hypoxic animal lung model?

Author Evidence Summary

The article screens 23 literature-derived disulfidptosis-related genes, identifies 12 differential genes and prioritizes NDUFS1, RPN1, TLN1, MYH10 and DSTN. Methods describe 25 control and 58 PH profiles in GSE117261, with the PH profiles divided into clusters of 48 and 10. Four models and a five-gene nomogram are evaluated; Figure 5 reports additional dataset AUCs of 0.761, 0.889 and 0.937. Dataset labels, model-task wording and the GLM AUC differ across article locations, retained at the affected results below. CIBERSORT and GSVA add relative immune/pathway context. A predicted ceRNA network and CTD/docking analysis nominate regulatory and folic-acid hypotheses. In the four-week hypoxia model, pulmonary arterial wall thickening and greater right ventricular pressure accompany higher MYH10 and lower DSTN, NDUFS1, RPN1 and TLN1 lung proteins. These complementary evidence roles support candidate selection and follow-up design.

Study Design & Analytical Framework

Study profile

Study Design
Retrospective public-expression analysis with candidate screening, consensus clustering, CIBERSORT, GSVA, SVM/RF/GLM/XGB comparisons, nomogram and additional dataset ROC analyses; predicted ceRNA/drug networks and docking; separate four-week hypoxic-rat experiment.
Evidence Type
Bulk-expression association; published model-evaluation summaries; inferred immune/pathway patterns; computational networks/docking; animal lung histology, right ventricular pressure and Western blot.
Population
Article-reported PH/control expression samples and additional PH-related datasets; male SPF animals described principally as rats allocated to control or hypoxic pulmonary hypertension groups.
Primary Endpoint
Prioritization and expression characterization of disulfidptosis-related candidates in PH.
Secondary Endpoint
Molecular clusters, reported model/nomogram evaluations, relative immune patterns, GSVA, predicted ceRNA/drug relationships, and animal lung/pressure/protein readouts.
External dataset evaluation
Yes

Data modalities

  • Public bulk gene expression
  • Inferred relative immune-cell proportions
  • GSVA pathway/function scores
  • Computational ceRNA and drug-target networks
  • Molecular docking
  • Animal lung histology and protein
  • Animal right ventricular pressure

What This Study Adds

  • Prioritizes a concrete five-gene PH candidate set and connects bulk expression with hypoxic-lung protein observations.
  • Provides separable expression-cluster, immune/pathway, model-evaluation and regulatory/drug-network evidence for literature comparisons.
  • Makes reported model estimates and computational folic-acid hypotheses citable with exact panel and source locations, while keeping their scientific roles explicit.

Evidence Scope

Supports

  • The article-reported expression differences and computational PH-profile clusters.
  • Published model estimates as source-specific summaries, including the discrepant labels and GLM value.
  • Predicted ceRNA/drug relationships, docking hypotheses and animal hypoxia-associated injury/protein directions.

Does Not Establish

  • A fully specified, independently validated clinical PH test or prospective decision benefit.
  • A causal role for the five genes or direct demonstration of disulfidptosis from expression follow-up.
  • Experimental folic-acid binding, target engagement or treatment efficacy in this study.

Limitations

  • Dataset identity differs between abstract, Methods and ROC Results: the source-specific accession mapping is retained in KF1/KF4; additional dataset group counts and analytical independence are not reconstructed.
  • Model task and partition wording differ between PH diagnosis, patient-cluster differentiation, training-data cross-validation and testing-cohort caption. Figure 4D gives GLM AUC 0.576 versus 0.756 in Results.
  • CIBERSORT estimates relative immune proportions. Results reverses the references to S4 and S5. The supplement captions describe S4 as pathway activity and S5 as biological function, while the corresponding images contain GO terms and KEGG pathways, respectively.
  • Animal allocation n=6/group and Western blot caption n=3 are different units. Assay reuse/replicate type are incompletely described, and one allocation sentence switches species terminology.
  • Expression follow-up and predicted ceRNA/drug networks supply hypotheses; candidate perturbation, direct disulfidptosis assays and folic-acid treatment effects were not tested.

Q&A

Which genes were prioritized and what human expression pattern was reported?

The five prioritized candidates are NDUFS1, RPN1, TLN1, MYH10 and DSTN. Figure 2 shows MYH10 higher and the other four lower in PH. Screening started from 23 literature-derived genes and identified 12 differential genes. Methods describes 25 control and 58 PH profiles in GSE117261; abstract accessions differ and are retained explicitly in KF1.

Evidence: KF1

What do the two molecular clusters represent?

They are computational clusters of the 58 PH expression profiles: C1 n=48 and C2 n=10. NDUFS1, DSTN and TLN1 are higher in C1; SLC7A11, LRPPRC, SLC3A2 and IQGAP1 are higher in C2. The clusters describe expression heterogeneity within the dataset.

Evidence: KF2

What immune and pathway evidence is available?

CIBERSORT estimates relative proportions of 22 immune-cell types. Figure S3 and Results show higher inferred proportions in C2 for follicular helper T cells (**), activated NK cells (*) and activated dendritic cells (**). The figure caption defines * P < 0.05 and ** P < 0.01. The actual S5 KEGG image contrasts metabolic pathways toward C2 with immune/signaling pathways toward C1; actual S4 contains GO terms. The article references those two figures in reverse order.

Evidence: KF2

What four-model performance can be cited?

Figure 4D reports XGB AUC 0.958, SVM 0.941, RF 0.853 and GLM 0.576. Results gives GLM 0.756 instead. Results describes fivefold evaluation in the training data for patient-cluster differentiation, whereas the caption says testing cohort and Methods uses PH diagnosis language. Cite these as the reported model comparison with those task/partition discrepancies.

Evidence: KF3

How are the additional ROC estimates mapped to datasets?

Read directly from Figure 5: D is GSE24988 PH, AUC 0.761 (95% CI 0.625-0.873); E is GSE33463 idiopathic PAH, 0.889 (0.809-0.957); F is GSE33463 scleroderma-associated PAH, 0.937 (0.880-0.980). The caption agrees with those panel labels, while Results assigns the latter two values to GSE24988 and GSE57345. The additional panels are described as fivefold cross-validation; a frozen independent clinical model is not demonstrated.

Evidence: KF4

What regulatory relationships were predicted?

The predicted ceRNA network contains 160 nodes and 187 edges, including five mRNAs, 40 miRNAs and 115 lncRNAs. It integrates TargetScan, miRDB, miRanda and SpongeScan predictions. The proposed LINC00689 links to RPN1 and DSTN offer candidates for experimental follow-up.

Evidence: KF5

What does the folic-acid result show?

CTD-based prediction links folic acid (D005492) to all five candidates, followed by docking poses in Figure 7B. Results reports a mean binding energy of -8.06 kcal/mol. The folic-acid analysis was limited to prediction and docking. The DOCX contains Table S2/S3 titles without individual drug/energy table bodies.

Evidence: KF6

What did the animal experiment actually measure?

Methods describes male SPF rats exposed to 10% oxygen for eight hours/day, six days/week, for four weeks, versus controls. Figure 8 shows pulmonary arterial wall thickening, greater right ventricular pressure, higher MYH10 lung protein and lower DSTN, NDUFS1, RPN1 and TLN1.

Evidence: KF7

How should animal sample sizes and assay units be described?

Methods allocates control n=6 and HPH n=6, while Figure 8C reports Western blot mean ± SD, n=3. Keep those units separate; the source does not fully explain assay reuse or biological-versus-technical replication. One allocation sentence says mice amid an otherwise rat-described model. The pressure plot labels the endpoint RVSP, measured by right-ventricular catheterization.

Evidence: KF7
