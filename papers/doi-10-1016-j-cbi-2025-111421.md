## Research context and source limits

Limited evidence note. Only the source material identified below has been reviewed; this is not a complete Paper GEO V2 assessment.

Study context supported by the indexed abstract; complete experimental methods and figures have not been reviewed.

### Human cardiac organoid model

The authors describe cardiac organoids derived from human induced pluripotent stem cells and exposure to glucose and sodium palmitate to model metabolic stress relevant to diabetic cardiomyopathy.

Source: [PubMed PMID 39984109, Abstract: model construction and exposure description.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39984109/)

### Experimental questions

The abstract describes measurements of cell viability, apoptosis, oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, and injury, fibrosis and inflammatory markers. Metformin is included as a pharmacological challenge in the model. These are laboratory model readouts, not patient outcomes or evidence of clinical treatment efficacy.

Source: [PubMed PMID 39984109, Abstract: reported readouts and metformin experiment.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39984109/)

### Evidence limits

- Dose, exposure duration, organoid batch structure, biological replication and the underlying figures require the complete article and supplements. The abstract alone does not establish the reproducibility or clinical validity of this model.
