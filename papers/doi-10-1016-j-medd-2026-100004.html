Research Question

How should cardiovascular multimodal AI account for incomplete or stale evidence when deciding whether to issue a prediction?

Author Evidence Summary

This correspondence separates conventional fusion, missing-modality robustness, temporal modeling, selective prediction and interpretation of why information is missing. It proposes representing observation state alongside patient state and assessing evidence sufficiency before producing a prediction. Figure 1 summarizes six principles. The authors suggest five candidate evaluation conditions, including incomplete inputs, natural co-missingness, temporal staleness and external data, and argue that deferral should lead to a predefined care pathway. This is a conceptual proposal that requires prospective evaluation and broader consensus; it is not a validated algorithm, clinical standard or demonstration of improved patient outcomes.
