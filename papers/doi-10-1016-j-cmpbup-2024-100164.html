Research question and data source

This study examines multimorbidity and frailty using NHANES survey cycles from 1999–2018. The paper describes cross-sectional analyses and machine-learning classification, rather than prospective prediction of new frailty or evaluation of a preventive treatment. Its scientific question concerns associations and classification within the analysed survey records.

Source: Methods 2: data source and participant selection; Methods 2.3

Frailty definition and analysis

Frailty is defined through a modified 36-item deficit-accumulation index. The Methods describe survey-weighted summaries, regression analyses and resampling for model selection. The complete item list and predictor overlap still require verification; reporting these procedures does not establish that outcome leakage was excluded or that an independent external cohort was tested.

Source: Methods 2.2: Assessment of disease and frailty; Methods 2.3; Supplementary materials

Unresolved analytical denominator

Methods reports 42,886 participants, while Results states 46,187 and then refers to 431 participants. Table 1 sex counts support a different total from the Results headline. These statements cannot be reconciled by selecting the most plausible number. The model-evaluation partition also remains insufficiently specified.

Source: Methods 2 first paragraph; Results 3 first paragraph; Table 1 Sex rows; Results 3.2
