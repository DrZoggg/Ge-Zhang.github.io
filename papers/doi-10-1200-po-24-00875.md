## Research Question

How do the authors justify their hybrid survival-model selection and feature-importance interpretation in response to methodological criticism?

## Author Evidence Summary

The reply describes an earlier framework combining 12 learners into 132 candidate modeling schemes, explains selection using average concordance across validation approaches, and discusses SHAP for feature interpretation. These are the authors' methodological arguments about prior studies. The reply does not add a new patient cohort, a new accuracy estimate, or an experiment demonstrating that SHAP removes bias. The original retrospective proctectomy study remains the source for its model results.
