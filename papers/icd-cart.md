### Review question

This review considers immunogenic cell death as a possible partner for CAR-T strategies in solid tumors. Its abstract connects stress-induced release of damage-associated molecular patterns with the immune environment and describes antigen heterogeneity and physical or immune barriers as challenges for CAR-T cells.

Source: [PubMed PMID 38936505, Abstract: opening rationale and solid-tumor barriers.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38936505/)

### Evidence level

The article is a review of mechanisms and proposed combination approaches, not a new clinical trial. Its discussion of potential synergy should be distinguished from demonstrated patient benefit for a specific regimen.

Source: [PubMed PMID 38936505, Abstract: final sentence identifying the review scope; publication type Review.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38936505/)
