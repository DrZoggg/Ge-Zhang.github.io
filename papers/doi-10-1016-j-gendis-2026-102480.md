## Evidence Base

**Trustworthy biomedical AI: data design, an evidence ladder and the deployment lifecycle**

This methodological review connects biomedical data provenance and endpoint design with model choice, interpretation, causal reasoning, validation and deployment. Its practical contributions are a disease-agnostic roadmap, a six-level evidence ladder and a proposed model-and-data card for reusable research and clinical translation.

## Research Question

How can biomedical AI studies connect heterogeneous data, appropriate models and interpretable findings to progressively stronger biological evidence and reliable real-world use?

## Author Evidence Summary

The review organizes biomedical AI across multi-omics, imaging and pathology, EHR and text, and longitudinal data. It links each modality’s data-generating process and endpoint choices to validity threats and model selection, covering classical and survival models, deep learning, self-supervised/foundation models, graph-guided learning, multimodal fusion and generative AI. Its evidence ladder distinguishes association, robustness, biological coherence, causal support, perturbation evidence and clinical utility. The evaluation sections connect leakage control, external and temporal validation, calibration, threshold-dependent decisions, uncertainty and subgroup behavior. The translation sections propose a model-and-data card and connect deployment with monitoring, drift detection, accountable updating and auditability. These are methodological proposals and synthesis, with no new data generated or analyzed.

## Q&A

### What is the main contribution and research genre?

It is a disease-agnostic methodological review and proposed conceptual framework. It connects data and endpoints to models, interpretation, causal reasoning, evaluation and deployment. The specific proposals include a six-level evidence ladder and a model-and-data card. The article reports no new data generation or analysis.

Evidence: KF1, KF5, KF7

### How does the framework help with heterogeneous biomedical data and endpoints?

It asks researchers to identify how each modality was generated and what the endpoint represents. Omics batch effects, imaging/site signatures, clinical care-process proxies and longitudinal timing create different validity threats. Operational prediction targets, mechanistic phenotypes, index times and prediction windows should be aligned with the question and intended use.

Evidence: KF2

### How are model families and multimodal choices compared?

The review covers classical/survival models, deep learning, self-supervised/foundation models, graph/knowledge-guided AI, multimodal fusion and generative AI. It relates each to its data and evaluation needs. Early, intermediate and late fusion differ in how they handle scales, missing modalities and cross-modal interactions; larger representations or attention weights do not supply validation on their own.

Evidence: KF3

### What distinguishes explanation from stronger mechanistic or causal support?

The review distinguishes population-level global explanations from case-level local explanations and recommends checking stability, biological coherence and orthogonal measurements. Pathway or cell-state summaries and spatial/tissue assays can help anchor model signals. Intervention questions require causal assumptions and aligned time zero, rather than interpreting feature importance as an intervention effect.

Evidence: KF4

### What are the six levels of the evidence ladder?

They are association, robustness, biological coherence, causal support, perturbation evidence and clinical utility. Each adds a different type of support: stability across reasonable analyses or cohorts; agreement with biological systems; assumption-aware intervention reasoning; experimental manipulation; and actionable decisions with benefit in realistic evaluation. The authors explicitly retain the value of studies at lower rungs when claims match their support.

Evidence: KF5

### What should evaluation add beyond a single discrimination metric?

The review connects leakage-safe splitting and preprocessing to external, cross-platform and temporal tests. Calibration, prediction horizons and decision-dependent thresholds complement ranking metrics. Missingness, perturbations, uncertainty under shift and subgroup performance help characterize where a model is reliable and how its outputs would affect decisions.

Evidence: KF6

### What does the proposed model-and-data card document?

It brings together provenance and inclusion criteria, time-anchored endpoints, splitting and preprocessing, intended population and horizon, validation, uncertainty, calibration, thresholds and subgroup performance. The review connects this documentation with workflow integration, version tracking, monitoring, drift detection, audit trails and accountable updating.

Evidence: KF7

It provides a methodological rationale for data and endpoint design, an evidence-strength discussion, and guidance for evaluation, reporting and deployment. The Cardiff repository Journal Pre-proof under the same formal DOI supplies the complete main text and five conceptual figure captions used for this summary.

Evidence: KF1, KF5, KF6, KF7
