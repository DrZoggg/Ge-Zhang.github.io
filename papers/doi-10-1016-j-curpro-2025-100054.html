Research Question

How can multimodal AI support cardiovascular mechanism research and updateable patient-specific digital models?

Author Evidence Summary

The Correspondence outlines two linked directions for cardiovascular AI: investigating disease mechanisms and developing patient-specific digital twins. It discusses combining imaging and molecular information to generate network and disease-subtyping hypotheses, then considers updateable physiological models, image-to-mesh workflows and treatment-scenario simulation. Longitudinal data, computational capacity, prospective evaluation, privacy and governance are presented as requirements for translation. This page organizes those narrative arguments for research planning and methodological citation. The cited empirical examples remain evidence from other works; cross-modal integration does not itself establish causation, and simulated treatment scenarios are not observed therapeutic effects in patients.

Does Not Establish

Limitations
