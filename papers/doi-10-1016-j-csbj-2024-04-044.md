## Research Question

How can proteomic measurement and integration with other omics contribute complementary information to precision oncology?

## Author Evidence Summary

This narrative review surveys mass spectrometry, aptamer-based assays, proximity extension assays, protein microarrays and combined single-cell methods. It explains how proteomic measurements and computational integration may reveal molecular subtypes, biomarkers, primary-tumor signatures and treatment-related biology. Figures 1-3 and Table 1 organize technologies, analysis and published application examples. The article is a synthesis of earlier research, so application-specific efficacy and accuracy estimates should be traced to their original studies. The review highlights costs, specimen availability and the need for integrated analysis as practical constraints.

## Evidence Scope

### Supports

- An overview of assay principles and integration strategies.
- Secondary contextualization of published precision-oncology examples.

### Does Not Establish

- A new patient cohort, pooled treatment benefit or meta-analytic accuracy estimate.
- Clinical superiority of one assay platform for every cancer or use case.
- Independent validation of all biomarkers discussed in cited primary studies.

### Limitations

- Narrative coverage does not establish a systematic or exhaustive evidence search.
- No pooled participant denominator or comparative effect estimate is reported.
- Translation depends on specimen availability, cost and application-specific validation.
