Research Question

What practical and technological approaches do the authors propose for dental care during prolonged space missions?

Author Evidence Summary

The authors propose three directions: strengthening dental skills within the crew, remotely guided care with virtual and 3D technologies, and adapting AI-driven robotics for autonomous treatment. They acknowledge crew constraints and communication delay or distortion. The letter cites prior literature and a commercial robotics example, but reports no astronaut cohort, microgravity experiment or quantitative evaluation of the proposed systems.
