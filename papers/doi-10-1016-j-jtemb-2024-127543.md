## Research context and source limits

Abstract-supported observational study context; full analytical methods and tables remain unavailable.

### Cross-sectional survey question

The study examines magnesium depletion score and depression in adults aged 20 to 60 years using NHANES 2005–2018. The indexed Methods specify PHQ-9 assessment of depression; the exposure is a depletion score rather than a randomized magnesium intervention.

Source: [PubMed PMID 39406123, Abstract: Background and Methods.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39406123/)

### Analysis and interpretation

The abstract describes multivariable logistic regression and restricted cubic splines. This design addresses associations measured in survey data. It does not establish that magnesium depletion causes depression or that supplementation would prevent or treat depression.

Source: [PubMed PMID 39406123, Abstract: Methods and Conclusions.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39406123/)

### Evidence limits

- The complete article is needed to verify survey weighting, exclusions, covariate definitions, missing-data handling and regression tables. Abstract-reported effect estimates are not presented here as independently checked full-text findings.
