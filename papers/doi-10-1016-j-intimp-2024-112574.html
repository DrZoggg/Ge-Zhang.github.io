Research Question

Which copper-related expression candidates characterize IC, how do their computational clusters and network contexts differ, and which protein directions recur in an injured mouse heart?

Author Evidence Summary

The authors screen 19 cuproptosis-related genes, identify 14 differentially expressed genes and nominate five candidates for computational and mouse follow-up. The paper reports 203 ischemic cardiomyopathy profiles and 152 control profiles after merging two GEO datasets. Its consensus analysis divides the 203 case profiles into groups of 174 and 29. The four-model comparison reports its highest AUC for SVM (0.914). Three additional panels give AUCs of 0.933, 0.800 and 0.932, alongside differences in sample accounting and evaluation descriptions across the source. Inferred immune and pathway profiles, a 218-node ceRNA network and a 370-node chemical–gene network provide follow-up hypotheses. A separate LAD-ligation experiment shows cardiac injury and the same directions for the five protein signals as the human expression comparison.

Evidence Scale

Cuproptosis-related IC candidates: expression, model comparisons and mouse follow-up

The study prioritizes DLST, ATP7B, FDX1, SLC31A1 and DLAT through cardiac-expression screening and a four-model comparison. In a separate mouse experiment, LAD ligation was associated with higher DLST and ATP7B protein signals and lower FDX1, SLC31A1 and DLAT signals amid cardiac injury. The paper reports different sample counts and model-evaluation descriptions across source locations.

Expression screen
19 literature-derived genes screened, 14 differentially expressed and 5 nominated.
Reported merged profiles
203 IC profiles and 152 controls. Figure 1 lists 181 and 91 IC profiles before merging.
Expression clusters
174 profiles in cluster 1 and 29 in cluster 2.
Additional dataset counts
Figure 1 displays 61 IC and 134 controls, 15 IC and 16 controls, and 12 IC and 5 controls. See KF4 for source-specific names.
Network sizes
218 nodes and 264 edges in the ceRNA network. 370 nodes and 615 edges in the chemical–gene network.
Mouse allocation and assay counts
6 allocated per group. Echocardiography caption n = 6. Western blot caption n = 4.

Counting note

Expression profiles, GEO accessions, inferred cell proportions and mouse assay sizes describe different units. The paper leaves the input-to-merged sample map and the fitted-model transfer procedure unresolved.

Study Design & Analytical Framework

Study profile

Study Design
Retrospective public cardiac-expression screening, consensus clustering, CIBERSORT/GSVA, four model comparisons and additional ROC analyses; predicted regulatory/chemical networks and docking; separate sham-versus-LAD-ligation mouse experiment.
Evidence Type
Bulk-expression association, reported model estimates, inferred relative immune/pathway patterns, predicted molecular networks/docking and animal injury/protein measurements.
Population
Public cardiac-expression profiles from ischemic cardiomyopathy and control groups. The separate animal experiment used eight-week-old male C57BL/6 mice.
Primary Endpoint
Nomination and expression characterization of five cuproptosis-related IC candidates.
Secondary Endpoint
Expression clusters, model and nomogram assessments, inferred immune and pathway patterns, predicted regulatory and chemical interactions, and mouse echocardiography, histology and protein expression.
External dataset evaluation
Yes

Data modalities

  • Bulk gene expression
  • Inferred relative immune proportions
  • GSVA
  • Model ROC/calibration/DCA
  • Predicted ceRNA/chemical networks
  • Molecular docking
  • Mouse echo/histology/proteins

What This Study Adds

  • Provides a concrete five-gene candidate set with public cardiac-expression context and separate mouse protein-expression observations.
  • Connects computational expression clusters and immune/pathway patterns with source-located model and nomogram comparisons.
  • Adds predicted regulatory and chemical-interaction maps that can inform follow-up experiments and comparison of evidence roles.

Evidence Scope

Supports

  • The article-reported candidate-expression patterns and computational cluster/network summaries.
  • Exact published ROC values attributed to their panels and original sample/evaluation descriptions.
  • Mouse injury and protein-expression directions in the sham-versus-ligation experiment.

Does Not Establish

  • A reconciled human sample map or frozen independent clinical diagnostic performance.
  • Gene perturbation effects or a direct experimental demonstration that these expression changes cause cuproptosis.
  • Chemical treatment benefit, measured molecular binding or a therapeutic ranking from the docking scores.

Limitations

  • The Figure 1 input counts, its GSE5345 label and the Abstract’s GSE42955 AUC differ from corresponding values elsewhere in the paper. A sample inclusion and exclusion map is unavailable.
  • Methods frames the model as identifying IC and Results describes cluster discrimination. The reported cross-validation within additional datasets and the deposited accession sets leave the actual partitions and transfer procedure unresolved.
  • The animal experiment measures cardiac injury and protein expression. The regulatory networks and docking results supply hypotheses for a different experimental question.
  • Mouse allocation, echocardiography and Western blot sample sizes refer to different reported units. Post-ligation sampling time and selection for each assay remain unspecified.
  • Supplementary Figures S1–S7 and Tables S1–S3 were unavailable. The source locators distinguish the main-text descriptions from the supporting plots and tables.

Q&A

Which five genes were nominated and what expression pattern was found?

DLST and ATP7B expression is higher in the human IC comparison, and FDX1, SLC31A1 and DLAT expression is lower. The authors select these five genes for the nomogram, network analyses and mouse follow-up after screening 19 genes and reporting 14 differentially expressed genes.

Evidence: KF1

What do the two molecular clusters describe?

The 203 reported IC profiles form two groups: 174 in cluster 1 and 29 in cluster 2. The groups differ in gene expression. Results §3.2 also reports differences in inferred immune proportions and pathway scores, including higher inferred M1 macrophage and neutrophil proportions in cluster 2.

Evidence: KF2

What four-model performance was reported?

Figure 4D reports AUCs of 0.914 for SVM, 0.911 for RF, 0.898 for XGB and 0.890 for GLM. The caption describes five-fold cross-validation in a testing cohort. Results describes IC-cluster differentiation, while Methods describes identification of IC.

Evidence: KF3

How should the additional AUC values be cited?

Figure 5D gives GSE42955 AUC 0.933 (95% CI 0.767–1.000). The Abstract instead gives 0.943. Figure 5E gives GSE48166 AUC 0.800 (95% CI 0.629–0.957), and Figure 5F gives GSE57345 AUC 0.932 (95% CI 0.892–0.965). The caption describes five-fold cross-validation within these datasets. The displayed Figure 5B is the decision curve, and Figure 5C is the calibration plot.

Evidence: KF3, KF4

Why are dataset membership and actual analysed samples kept separate?

GSE57338 deposits 313 unique GEO sample accessions, all present among the 319 in GSE57345. Figure 1’s two input case counts, 181 and 91, sum to 272, while the reported merged count is 203. Figure 1 also prints GSE5345 where Methods and the Figure 5 caption name GSE57345. The actual sample-selection map remains unresolved.

Evidence: KF1, KF4

What does the ceRNA analysis add?

The predicted network has 218 nodes and 264 edges, comprising 5 mRNAs, 52 miRNAs and 161 long noncoding RNAs. It proposes regulatory links around the five candidates, including associations involving LINC00969.

Evidence: KF5

What did chemical prediction and docking evaluate?

The CTD network contains 365 chemicals, 5 genes and 615 edges. The authors report eight entries associated with all five genes and exclude vehicle emissions from docking because a structure was unavailable. Figure 7B shows the benzo(a)pyrene–FDX1 docking score of −11.3 kcal/mol. Panels C–G show tretinoin docking poses. The mouse experiment did not administer these chemicals.

Evidence: KF6

Which observations came from the mouse experiment?

In mice with LAD ligation, histology shows myocardial injury, echocardiography shows lower ejection fraction and fractional shortening and greater end-diastolic diameter, and protein assays show higher DLST and ATP7B and lower FDX1, SLC31A1 and DLAT than in controls. Methods allocates six per group. The echo and Western blot captions report n = 6 and n = 4, respectively.

Evidence: KF7

Where can this work contribute to a research manuscript?

The work provides background on copper-related expression patterns in ischemic cardiomyopathy and a methods example linking public expression screening, model comparisons, inferred immune proportions and mouse protein assays. Cite the specific panel and dataset for a model estimate, and the actual mouse assay for an expression observation.
