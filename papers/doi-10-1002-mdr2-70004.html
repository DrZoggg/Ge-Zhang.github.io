Official Abstract

The progression of differentiated thyroid carcinoma (DTC) poses significant clinical challenges, especially in determining the optimal time for intervention. To capture early signals of disease progression, we employed an optimized dynamic network biomarker (DNB) method—a systems biology approach that detects abrupt molecular changes indicating a critical transition signal. This analysis revealed that Stage II marks a critical transition in the disease trajectory. We further developed a scoring system called TCPSLevel (Thyroid Carcinoma Progression Signature Level), which quantifies individual progression risk based on gene expression profiles. TCPSLevel showed strong associations with clinical features and prognosis across multiple datasets. Our ensemble consensus clustering approach uncovered three robust DTC molecular subtypes, which demonstrated distinct clinical outcomes, immune microenvironments, regulatory landscapes, and therapeutic agents. A clinically applicable classifier (miniPC) was constructed using machine learning to facilitate subtype prediction. We also identified ASPH as a key regulator driving progression and validated its expression and function experimentally. Together, these findings offer new insights and practical tools for early risk assessment and personalized management of thyroid cancer.

Counting note

TCGA counts of 544 in Methods and 484 in GRN analysis describe different reported analytical stages; no reason for excluding the numerical difference is inferred. Methods reports 17,709 cells and Results 17,518, without a reconstructed QC-exclusion process. One reported donor is not a large participant cohort; 48 tumor and 48 adjacent specimens do not mean 96 independent patients. Cohorts, cells, paired specimens and cultured-cell experiments are not added into a unique-participant total. External validation means retrospective expression-template/classifier evaluation, not prospective clinical utility.

When This Study Is Useful to Cite

  1. CU1: Which thyroid studies applied dynamic network biomarkers to stage-associated expression?

    Stage II had the highest reported network instability signal. DNB genes: 36. Cross-sectional stage comparisons do not establish longitudinal prediction of each patient's future deterioration time. TCPSLevel was associated with clinical and outcome differences. Expression bins: 100; Background samplings: 1,000; Reported survival cutoff: 0.4. The reported cutoff is not a cross-cohort locked clinical threshold. Grouping rules used in other analyses are not automatically equivalent.

    Evidence: KF1, KF2

  2. CU2: What evidence supports ensemble thyroid-cancer molecular subtyping?

    The ensemble analysis yielded three molecular subtypes. Candidate combinations: 20; Selected combination: CV-PAM; Reported external NTP datasets: 6. Methods 2.6 prints GSE333630 whereas other locations use GSE33630. These are not silently rewritten as a fully verified accession inventory.

    Evidence: KF3

  3. CU3: How was a compact classifier developed for consensus thyroid subtypes?

    miniPC classified the consensus subtype labels using a compact gene set. Candidate genes: 296; Final genes: 12; Final classifier: Neural network; External classifier cohorts: 5. This is subtype classification, not prospective individual survival-benefit validation. Unverified figure-only accuracy or AUC values are not reconstructed.

    Evidence: KF4

  4. CU4: What evidence distinguishes ASPH expression association from experimental perturbation?

    Single-cell analysis localized expression patterns across cellular compartments. Reported donors: 1; Cells in Methods: 17,709; Cells in Results: 17,518; Annotated populations: 7. A single donor and a large cell count do not establish large-population replication. No explanation for the count difference is invented. ASPH was evaluated in human tissues and through TPC-1 silencing. Tumor specimens: 48; Adjacent specimens: 48; TPC-1 silencing: Reduced proliferation and migration. Paired tissues are not 96 independent patients. ASCL2-ASPH network inference is not direct binding validation, and cell effects do not establish human targeted-treatment efficacy.

    Evidence: KF5, KF6

  5. CU5: What computational rather than clinical therapeutic hypotheses were generated?

    IPS and CMap supplied candidate immune/drug-response hypotheses. Evidence: Computational scoring and signature matching. Predicted compounds are not proven effective treatments. IPS is not an observed outcome in patients receiving immunotherapy.

    Evidence: KF7

Q&A

What does the Stage II DNB signal establish?

Stage II had the highest reported network instability signal. DNB genes: 36. Cross-sectional stage comparisons do not establish longitudinal prediction of each patient's future deterioration time.

Evidence: KF1

Is TCPSLevel already a clinically validated prospective risk tool?

TCPSLevel was associated with clinical and outcome differences. Expression bins: 100; Background samplings: 1,000; Reported survival cutoff: 0.4. The reported cutoff is not a cross-cohort locked clinical threshold. Grouping rules used in other analyses are not automatically equivalent.

Evidence: KF2

How were the thyroid molecular subtypes evaluated externally?

The ensemble analysis yielded three molecular subtypes. Candidate combinations: 20; Selected combination: CV-PAM; Reported external NTP datasets: 6. Methods 2.6 prints GSE333630 whereas other locations use GSE33630. These are not silently rewritten as a fully verified accession inventory.

Evidence: KF3

Does miniPC classify subtypes or directly prove improved survival?

miniPC classified the consensus subtype labels using a compact gene set. Candidate genes: 296; Final genes: 12; Final classifier: Neural network; External classifier cohorts: 5. This is subtype classification, not prospective individual survival-benefit validation. Unverified figure-only accuracy or AUC values are not reconstructed.

Evidence: KF4

How many independent donors support the single-cell analysis?

Single-cell analysis localized expression patterns across cellular compartments. Reported donors: 1; Cells in Methods: 17,709; Cells in Results: 17,518; Annotated populations: 7. A single donor and a large cell count do not establish large-population replication. No explanation for the count difference is invented.

What human and experimental evidence supports ASPH?

Were CMap-nominated compounds shown to benefit patients?
