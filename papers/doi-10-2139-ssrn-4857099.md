### Abstract-level study design

The abstract describes a retrospective multicentre comparison of sleeve lobectomy and lobectomy after neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy in central-type NSCLC. It reports intraoperative margin assessment and propensity-score weighting. Full eligibility rules, treatment allocation, tables, and follow-up data were not available for verification.

Source: [Abstract: Methods](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4857099)

### Clinical evidence boundary
