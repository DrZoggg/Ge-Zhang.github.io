## Evidence Scale

**Deciphering lung adenocarcinoma evolution: Integrative single-cell genomics identifies the prognostic lung progression associated signature**

Integrative single-cell and bulk-transcriptomic analyses nominated a Cluster 6-related lung progression-associated signature (LPAS) associated with survival and inferred immune states in lung adenocarcinoma. PSMB1 silencing reduced proliferative and migratory phenotypes in cultured LUAD cells; these findings do not establish patient treatment efficacy.

| Evidence | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Single-cell specimen scale | GSE189357 and GSE150938: 4 AIS, 8 MIA and 9 IAC specimens (21 reported across pathology groups) |
| Independent survival-series list | GSE13213 (119), GSE29016 (39), GSE30219 (86), GSE31210 (227) and GSE68465 (443) as reported analyzed series |
| Model discovery | TCGA-LUAD; cohort n not inferred from unspecified filtering |
| Local stage-associated RNA sequencing | 14 LUAD surgical specimens (reported) |
| LPAS signature | 9 genes |

### Counting note

The 21 scRNA-seq specimens, 14 local RNA-seq specimens, TCGA cases, five survival cohorts and mixed-cancer immunotherapy datasets represent distinct analytic layers; neither cross-dataset independence nor a synthetic unique-patient total is inferred.

## Research Question

Can cell-state patterns associated with adenocarcinoma in situ, minimally invasive adenocarcinoma and invasive lung adenocarcinoma inform a prognostic expression signature, and which experimental observations support prioritized genes?

## Author Evidence Summary

The researchers integrated two public single-cell LUAD series, TCGA transcriptomic and survival data, five independent GEO survival series, exploratory immunotherapy-response datasets and cultured-cell knockdown assays. The prognostic evidence is observational; pseudotime, immune scores and therapy-response computations require separate interpretation from experiments.

## Study Design & Model Development

### Study profile

- Study Design: Secondary integration of human LUAD single-cell and bulk expression cohorts with retrospective survival modeling, computational immune/drug-response inference and in-vitro PSMB1 perturbation.
- Evidence Type: Human transcriptomic association, retrospective model evaluation and LUAD cell-line functional assays.
- Population: Early pathological LUAD tissue samples across AIS, MIA and IAC; independent retrospective GEO/TCGA LUAD survival cohorts and additional tumor-treatment series from heterogeneous cancers.
- Primary Endpoint: Association of the LPAS expression score with overall survival in retrospective LUAD series.
- Secondary Endpoint: Single-cell state contrasts across pathological stages; immune/stromal signature associations; computational immunotherapy/drug-response hypotheses; PSMB1 knockdown cell phenotypes.
- External dataset evaluation: Yes
- Data modalities: Two publicly available LUAD scRNA-seq series, TCGA and GEO transcriptomic and clinical survival data, Exploratory external treatment-response expression datasets, Local LUAD RNA sequencing, A549 and H1299 siRNA perturbation assays

### Model development

- Model Name: Lung progression-associated signature (LPAS)
- Model Family: Nine-gene expression-based survival model
- Development: TCGA-LUAD retrospective transcriptomic and survival data
- External Evaluation: Five GEO LUAD retrospective survival cohorts; horizon-specific AUCs vary.
- Clinical Boundary: No prospective LPAS-guided clinical decision validation.

## What This Study Adds

- Connects an early-LUAD epithelial-state signature to multi-cohort retrospective survival risk stratification.
- Adds cell-line perturbation observations for PSMB1 while maintaining distinct levels of molecular association and function.

## Evidence Scope

### Supports

- Cluster 6 and LPAS show reproducible associations in the reported transcriptomic cohorts.
- Higher LPAS corresponds to less favorable retrospective LUAD survival in the evaluated cohorts.
- PSMB1 silencing reduces selected proliferation and movement phenotypes in A549/H1299 assays.

### Does Not Establish

- A directly traced longitudinal path from an AIS cell to an IAC cell or a causal origin of Cluster 6.
- Independent prospective diagnostic, treatment selection, or outcome improvement from LPAS.
- Clinical benefit from PSMB1 inhibition, LPAS-guided immunotherapy or computationally prioritized compounds.
- Unique participant counts aggregated across partially overlapping or heterogeneous public datasets.

### Limitations

- Early stages and bulk outcome datasets are distinct study populations; pseudotime is not lineage tracing.
- Source dataset descriptions include heterogeneous immunotherapy studies, and the article uses different counting contexts rather than a single combined n.
- Single-cell immune estimates and simulated drug sensitivity are not direct measurements of clinical response.
- PSMB1 siRNA provides selected in-vitro evidence, not an in-vivo drug efficacy or clinical trial.

## Q&A

### Which pathological stages were compared in the single-cell analysis?

Four AIS, eight MIA and nine IAC specimen profiles were described across two public scRNA-seq series. They are specimens at different pathological stages, not serial samples tracking the same person.

Evidence: KF1

### What is the nine-gene LPAS model?

LPAS is a retrospective expression-based LUAD survival score containing nine selected genes, including PSMB1. It was developed from TCGA data and evaluated in five additional GEO survival series.

Evidence: KF3

### Was LPAS externally evaluated?

Five additional GEO LUAD survival datasets were evaluated, with differing reported AUCs and follow-up horizons. This is retrospective external dataset evaluation, not a prospective treatment-impact trial.

Evidence: KF3

### Does inferred cluster progression prove actual tumor-cell fate transitions?

No. InferCNV, changing cluster frequencies and pseudotime reconstruct possible molecular patterns from cross-sectional data; they do not directly track cells through AIS, MIA and IAC.

Evidence: KF1, KF2

### Can low LPAS identify patients who will benefit from immunotherapy?

The article reports in-silico immune scores and SubMap/TIDE comparisons plus treatment-cohort associations, but no prospective LPAS-guided treatment study establishes patient-level clinical benefit.

Evidence: KF4

### What did the PSMB1 experiments establish?

PSMB1 siRNA reduced cell growth, colony formation and motility-related outcomes in A549/H1299 culture experiments. It does not establish in-vivo therapeutic efficacy or a validated drug target in patients.

Evidence: KF5
