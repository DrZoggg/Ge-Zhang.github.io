Research Question

Does the combination of public expression data, genetic association, tissue staining and A549 perturbation support TGFBR2 as a candidate for further lung adenocarcinoma research?

Author Evidence Summary

The study integrated TCGA-LUAD and two GEO expression datasets with feature-selection methods, identifying four candidate genes. TGFBR2 had a nominal inverse MR association in the reported four-instrument analysis. The actual Figure 8 images and quantification show lower TGFBR2 staining in five LUAD specimens than in three control specimens, consistent with the Results paragraph and abstract but contrary to that subsection heading. In A549 cells, TGFBR2 siRNA increased CCK8 signal and Transwell migration and reduced apoptosis. These evidence components are kept separate: they do not establish diagnostic accuracy in clinical practice, universal protective causality, or drug efficacy.

Counting note

Public expression validation does not mean prospective clinical validation. Tissue specimens, GWAS samples, SNP instruments and culture wells are different units and are not summed. Triplicate CCK8 wells are not three independent patient samples; an independent biological-replicate count is not inferred for all assays.

What This Study Adds

  • Connects public-data gene prioritization with a directly inspected tissue comparison and a separate cell-perturbation experiment.
  • Provides experimental reasons to study TGFBR2 while keeping nominal MR evidence, expression differences and cell functions distinct from clinical validation.

Evidence Scope

Supports

  • A reported nominal inverse TGFBR2 MR association in the specified analysis.
  • Lower TGFBR2-positive staining in the eight-specimen IHC comparison.
  • The specified A549 knockdown-associated assay responses.

Does Not Establish

  • Unconditional causality or universal protection against LUAD from a nominal selected-gene MR association.
  • Reliable diagnostic, prognostic or treatment-selection performance in clinical practice.
  • Drug efficacy or safety from docking predictions.
  • That all MR sensitivity results are free of unresolved direction or source-table labeling issues.

Limitations

  • Results 3.7 is headed increased TGFBR2 expression, while its paragraph, the abstract and Figure 8 show decreased expression. This page explicitly attributes the lower staining direction to the latter sources; no formal correction was found in the current publisher page or JATS links.
  • The forward MR result is nominal and comes from a multi-stage selection pipeline. It is not an unconditional causal conclusion or clinical biomarker validation.
  • The main text cites Supplementary Table S2 for forward heterogeneity and pleiotropy values, but the downloaded S2 contains LUAD-to-TGFBR2 exposure/outcome labels and different Q-test P values. This page does not claim that those sensitivity results validate absence of heterogeneity or pleiotropy.
  • The tissue comparison is small: five LUAD and three control specimens. It does not establish generalizable diagnostic performance.
  • Functional testing used A549 only. Triplicate wells in the CCK8 setup are not independent donors; the number of independent biological replicates is not stated uniformly across the reported cell assays.
  • The paper reports computational drug predictions. No clinical drug efficacy or safety is established.

    The forward IVW analysis used four SNPs and reported beta -0.059814, SE 0.02863 and P=0.0366879 in Supplementary Table S1, rounded to beta -0.06 and P=0.04 in the main text. This is nominal evidence within a candidate-selection pipeline and remains dependent on MR assumptions.

    Evidence: KF1

  2. CU2: Does the tissue figure show higher or lower TGFBR2 in LUAD?

    Figure 8 shows lower TGFBR2-positive staining in five LUAD specimens than in three controls. The abstract and Results paragraph agree; the Results 3.7 heading says increased and is inconsistent with that evidence. The heading has not been formally corrected in the sources checked.

    Evidence: KF2

  3. CU3: What did the A549 perturbation experiment show?

    After TGFBR2 knockdown, CCK8 and Transwell-migration readouts increased and the apoptosis readout decreased. Two siRNAs were screened, and the more effective sequence was selected for subsequent assays. These results are from one cultured-cell model.

    Evidence: KF3

Q&A

How strong is the reported TGFBR2 MR evidence?

The forward IVW analysis used four SNPs and reported beta -0.059814, SE 0.02863 and P=0.0366879 in Supplementary Table S1, rounded to beta -0.06 and P=0.04 in the main text. This is nominal evidence within a candidate-selection pipeline and remains dependent on MR assumptions.

Evidence: KF1

Does the tissue figure show higher or lower TGFBR2 in LUAD?

Figure 8 shows lower TGFBR2-positive staining in five LUAD specimens than in three controls. The abstract and Results paragraph agree; the Results 3.7 heading says increased and is inconsistent with that evidence. The heading has not been formally corrected in the sources checked.

Evidence: KF2

What did the A549 perturbation experiment show?

After TGFBR2 knockdown, CCK8 and Transwell-migration readouts increased and the apoptosis readout decreased. Two siRNAs were screened, and the more effective sequence was selected for subsequent assays. These results are from one cultured-cell model.

Evidence: KF3

Can these findings guide LUAD diagnosis or treatment now?

The study provides candidate-gene evidence, not clinical implementation evidence. A nominal MR association, eight tissue specimens and A549 assays do not establish patient-level diagnostic utility, treatment benefit or drug safety.
