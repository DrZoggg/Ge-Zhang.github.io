Research context and source limits

Limited evidence note. Only the source material identified below has been reviewed; this is not a complete Paper GEO V2 assessment.

Clinical study context reported in the indexed abstract; patient-level tables and comparative outcomes have not been audited.

Retrospective clinical series

The abstract describes consecutive patients with esophageal gastrointestinal stromal tumors treated at one hospital between January 2017 and August 2023. It reports 23 patients and assessment of clinical characteristics and long-term quality of life.

Source: PubMed PMID 38968857, Abstract: Methods and Results, overall cohort.

Treatment-selection question

The series includes endoscopic resection, surgery, surgery after preoperative imatinib, and conservative management. These are observed management choices in a small retrospective series, not randomized treatment allocations or proof that one strategy is superior.

Source: PubMed PMID 38968857, Abstract: Results, treatment categories; Conclusions, study limitations.

Evidence limits

  • The full article is required to verify patient selection, tumor characteristics, treatment eligibility, follow-up and quality-of-life instruments. Comparative effectiveness or safety cannot be established from the abstract-level counts.
