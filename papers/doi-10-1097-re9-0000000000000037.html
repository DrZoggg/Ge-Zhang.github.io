Author evidence note

Review question

This review examines how microbial processes and host characteristics can be combined to study cardiovascular disease, separating vascular and cardiac pathways.

Source: Publisher HTML, Abstract, sole paragraph, sentences 1-5.

What the review contributes

It organizes integration of microbial profiles, genomics and metabolomics with imaging and repeated clinical observations, and compares approaches for combining these data. A central distinction is between support for a mechanism and the maturity of a biomarker. The review prioritizes reproducible cardiovascular subtypes and microbial candidates for subsequent intervention studies.

Source: Publisher HTML, Abstract, sole paragraph, sentences 5-7 and 10.

Research use

This synthesis can inform background writing, multimodal study design and discussion of biomarker translation. Its abstract places current support more firmly in biological discovery than clinical deployment, and calls for confounder control, comparisons against clinical and single modality models, external validation and prospective evaluation of clinical usefulness.
