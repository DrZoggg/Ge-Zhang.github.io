Sources and citation

This author-prepared map derives from the sample metadata before the expression table in the official GSE59867 series-matrix file, retrieved on 15 November 2026. Source fields are !Sample_geo_accession, !Sample_title and the collection, HF-progression and cell-type rows of !Sample_characteristics_ch1. The CSV contains metadata rather than expression values.

Original dataset publication. GSE59867 in GEO links PMID 25984239 and BioProject PRJNA256954. Cite the original study and GEO record for the dataset.

Published analyses using GSE59867. These papers analyze the archived series for different questions. Cite the original study and GEO record for the samples, and the relevant paper for its analysis.

  • Plaque-vulnerability signature development. The APVS study used GSE59867 for STEMI versus chronic coronary syndrome gene filtering and included it in its development and internal test split (STAR Methods and Fig. S1B–C).
  • Molecular subtype assignment. The smooth muscle cell fate study applied discovery-derived subtype templates to GSE59867 PBMC profiles (nearest-template prediction and Fig. 6C).
  • Circadian-related expression subtyping. The ischemic heart disease study reused this series for retrospective molecular subtyping. The GEO collection fields record time since infarction.
  • Admission-only diagnostic comparison. A cross-cohort study compared 111 STEMI admission samples with 46 stable coronary artery disease samples and excluded repeated visits from its diagnostic training comparison (Methods §§2.1 and 2.4).
  • Longitudinal expression analysis. A recovery-trajectory study analyzed serial samples from 81 eligible STEMI participants. Its recovery grouping was defined from gene expression (Methods and Figs. 1–3).
